name: Weeping Halls
tileset: Cathedral
seed: 4102
note: Six long galleries in a switchback, with doors through the walls as shortcuts. The party takes every shortcut unless locked out, so an open keep is a straight dash. A lock near a turn sends them round; on a door far from the turn the way round costs more than the lock, so they stop and break it, and stand where the player wants them while they do.
